- Official caption/reference: [The Blue Marble: The View From Apollo 17](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/blue-marble-view-from-apollo-17/).

- NASA media use guidance: <https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-web-privacy-policy-important-notices/>.

The downloaded photograph is used for the Earth disc only: cropped, resized and softly masked
into an original lunar landscape. NASA imagery is generally public domain, subject to NASA's
use guidance; no NASA insignia, endorsement or affiliation is claimed. No recognizable people
appear. Credit is also displayed in the game's Credits screen.

The composite is **an artistic game backdrop**, not a NASA lunar-site photograph, physically
scaled Earthrise reconstruction or claim about lunar gravity. Lunar landscape, stars, shading
and terrain textures are original code-authored art. The JPEG/lossless masters are development
sources; the client ships only the optimized locally bundled WebP composite.
